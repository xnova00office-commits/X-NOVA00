Privacy Policy for X-NOVA00

Last updated: September 2, 2026

X-NOVA00 ("we", "our", or "us") provides cloud-based digital attendance management solutions designed to streamline operational workflows. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users' information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application accesses, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information in compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

1. Information We Access and Collect

Our application integrates directly with Google Workspace services to provide attendance tracking features. We access the following information strictly to execute core app functionalities:

  • Google User Account Information: Basic profile information (such as email address) utilized solely for account authentication, user identification, and operational logging.
  • Google Drive / Spreadsheet Data: Through the authorized https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, the application accesses, creates, reads, and appends records strictly within specific Google Spreadsheets that you explicitly select or that are generated directly by the application for attendance tracking purposes.

We do not access, scan, or read any unauthorized files, personal emails, or spreadsheets unrelated to the application.

2. Use of Google User Data

We strictly use Google user data for the following essential purposes:

  • Authenticating user identity to ensure secure punch-in and punch-out operations.
  • Recording timecard timestamps, branch locations, and authorized PIN verifications into designated master spreadsheets.
  • Generating monthly dynamic summary tables and attendance reports requested by administrators.

We adhere strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements, develop generalized AI models, or transfer data to third parties.

3. Data Protection and Security Measures

We implement rigorous technical and administrative security safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your information:

  • In-Transit Encryption: All communications and API calls between the application and Google servers are encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • Direct Architecture (No External Storage): All attendance records and operational configurations are stored directly within your designated Google Drive / Google Sheets environment. We do not store, duplicate, or mirror your operational records on external private databases.
  • Access Control: System administrative configurations are restricted exclusively to authorized administrative users defined within your master configuration sheet.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

We ensure users maintain absolute control over their operational and profile data:

Data Retention:

  • Attendance punch records and employee logs are retained directly within your user-managed Google Sheets for as long as your organization maintains the document.
  • Session tokens and temporary authorization credentials are stored in memory or user-isolated storage and automatically expire according to standard OAuth protocol lifetimes.

Data Deletion Procedures:

Users and administrators can delete their data or revoke access at any time through the following steps:

  1. In-App Data Deletion: Administrators can delete individual punch records, employee lists, or entire monthly summary sheets directly within their Google Sheets at their sole discretion.
  2. Access Revocation: You may revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time by navigating to your Google Account Security Settings and selecting "Remove Access" for X-NOVA00. Once revoked, the application can no longer access or modify any files.
  3. Data Deletion Request: To request complete removal of any operational logs, setup inquiries, or support communication records held by us, please send an explicit deletion request email to xnova00.office@gmail.com. All corresponding records will be permanently deleted from our communication logs within 30 business days.

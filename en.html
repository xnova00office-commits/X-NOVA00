X-NOVA00 Time Card

Track employee attendance with smartphone QR codes across up to two locations, with support for up to 50 employees. Attendance records go directly into Google Sheets.

Contact us View on Google Workspace Marketplace

Features

  • Clock in and out by scanning a QR code on a smartphone.
  • Calculate work hours and monthly totals automatically, including overnight shifts.
  • Verify the location with GPS when employees clock in or out.
  • Let administrators review and approve requests to correct time records.

What you need

Administrators
A Google account for sign-in and managing attendance data in Google Sheets.
Employees
A smartphone with internet access and location permission. Standard QR clock-in and clock-out do not require a Google account.
